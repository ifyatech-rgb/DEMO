# Reference ad style (ref1/assets/ref.mp4, 30 s, 9:16)

Script: "If you've received a non-renewal notice from your insurance or your premiums are skyrocketing, you need an
expert to help you navigate it. ... So book your 30-minute inspection to get your roof analyzed for insurance
compliance and save your coverage."  (timed transcript: ref-transcript.json)

1. Opening (0-0.4 s): "sketch reveal": the speaker appears as a white line drawing over the real background,
   fills into a glowing white ghost, then snaps to full colour. Background stays normal throughout.
2. Hook header, 0 -> first cut (~4.7 s): two white rounded text boxes at the very top, black bold text,
   line 1 big ("DFY HOMEOWNERS,"), line 2 smaller ("read your mail!"). TikTok-native text-box look. Static.
3. Captions: condensed bold sans (Bebas/Anton style), ALL CAPS, white, no outline, soft shadow; 2-4 words;
   the spoken word turns yellow (#FFD400-ish); sits mid-frame (~48 % height, around his waist/hands).
4. Emphasis captions on key lines ("YOU NEED AN EXPERT", "TO HELP YOU NAVIGATE IT"): much bigger, 2 lines,
   white with the key word in green, same place, word-by-word build.
5. One photo card: a real photo matching the line (the non-renewal letter), rounded corners + thin white border,
   lower-middle, over his legs; pops in on the keyword (~1 s), leaves at the cut with a quick blur + drop.
6. Cuts: jump cuts every 1.2-4 s between a wide (full body) and a closer framing (8 cuts in 30 s).
   Camera is otherwise static: no zooms, shakes, whips or flashes.
7. End CTA from "So book your..." to the last frame: dark gradient over the bottom ~40 %, small white
   "BOOK YOUR", big green condensed "30-MINUTE / INSPECTION", three green down-arrows; captions keep running above.
8. Sound: voice only; no music or sound effects detected (no steady beat; pauses carry room/outdoor tone).
9. Look: natural, bright daylight, no heavy grade. Clean and simple: at most one graphic on screen at a time.
